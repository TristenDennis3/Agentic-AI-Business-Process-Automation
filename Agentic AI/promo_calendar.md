# Promotional Calendar and Blackout Windows

Northwind Outdoor Co., Marketing Operations. Q4 2026.

Per pricing policy section 5, no list price change may take effect during a blackout window.

## Blackout windows

| Window | Dates | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall Clearance | 2026-09-15 to 2026-10-05 | Active promotion, closed |
| Holiday Build | 2026-11-01 to 2026-12-31 | Peak selling season, price locked |
| Spring Reset | 2027-03-01 to 2027-03-21 | Catalog refresh |

## Open window

**2026-10-06 to 2026-10-31.** List price changes approved during this period would ordinarily take effect on the first business day of the following month, which is 2026-11-02.

That date falls inside the Holiday Build blackout, which runs through 2026-12-31. The next available effective date is therefore the first business day after the blackout ends.

2027-01-01 is a Friday and a holiday. 2027-01-02 and 2027-01-03 fall on the weekend. **The first available effective date is Monday 2027-01-04.**
