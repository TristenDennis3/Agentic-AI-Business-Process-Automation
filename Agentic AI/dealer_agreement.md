# Authorized Dealer Agreement

Northwind Outdoor Co. and Authorized Dealer. Form NW-DA-2026. Excerpt for pricing review purposes.

---

## Section 3. Pricing and Resale

**3.1** Dealer purchases Products at the wholesale price schedule in effect on the date of order.

**3.2** Dealer sets its own resale prices and nothing in this Agreement obligates Dealer to resell at any particular price, subject only to the advertising terms in Section 4.

---

## Section 4. Minimum Advertised Price

**4.1** "Minimum Advertised Price," or MAP, means the lowest price at which a Product may be publicly advertised. MAP for each Product is eighty-five percent (85%) of the then-current published list price.

**4.2** Northwind covenants that it will not advertise, offer, or promote any Product through any Northwind-controlled channel, including its direct-to-consumer website, at a price below that Product's MAP. Northwind acknowledges that Dealer has relied on this covenant in committing to minimum annual purchase volumes under Section 6.

**4.3** A breach of Section 4.2 entitles Dealer, at its sole election, to terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days written notice and to be released from the minimum purchase commitments in Section 6 without penalty.

**4.4** MAP applies to advertised price only. It does not restrict the price at which a Product is actually sold.

**4.5** Products designated by Northwind as direct-to-consumer exclusive are not subject to this Section.

---

## Section 5. Price Change Notice

**5.1** Northwind shall provide Dealer no less than thirty (30) days written notice before any change to the published list price of a Product carried by Dealer.

**5.2** Orders accepted prior to the effective date of a price change are honored at the prior price.

---

## Section 6. Volume Commitment

**6.1** Dealer commits to minimum annual purchase volumes set out in Schedule B.

**6.2** Dealer's commitment under this Section is expressly conditioned on Northwind's compliance with Section 4.2.

---

## Section 9. Term and Termination

**9.1** This Agreement has an initial term of twenty-four (24) months and renews annually unless either party gives sixty (60) days written notice.

**9.2** Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

**9.3** The termination right in Section 4.3 is in addition to, and not in limitation of, the rights in this Section.

---

*Counterparties under this form as of 2026-10-01: Mountain Supply Co., Lakeshore Outfitters, Great Lakes Gear Exchange.*
