# Pricing Policy

Northwind Outdoor Co., Commercial Finance. Revision 7.1, effective 2026-07-01.

## 1. Definitions

**Landed cost** means the ex-works unit cost plus inbound freight plus duty and tariff. All margin calculations in this policy are performed on landed cost.

**Gross margin** means (selling price minus landed cost) divided by selling price, expressed as a percentage.

**Comparable price** means the competitor's price for the quantity Northwind sells as one unit, inclusive of shipping. Where the competitor's pack quantity differs from Northwind's, the competitor's listed price is scaled by (Northwind units_per_sale divided by competitor pack quantity). A set is compared to a set and a single unit to a single unit.

## 2. Margin floors by product line

No selling price may be set below the floor for its product line.

| Product line | Minimum gross margin |
| --- | --- |
| Lighting | 42% |
| Hydration | 42% |
| Sleep | 40% |
| Storage | 40% |
| Cooking | 38% |

A proposed price that breaches its floor requires a written margin exception approved under section 6.

## 3. Minimum advertised price

Products sold through the dealer channel are subject to a minimum advertised price. MAP is set at 85% of the **then-current** published list price and recalculates whenever list price changes.

MAP constrains advertised discounting below list. It does not constrain the list price itself, since a list price is by definition above 85% of itself.

**3.1 Advertised discounts.** No Northwind-controlled channel, including direct-to-consumer, may advertise a dealer-channel product below its current MAP. Doing so places Northwind in breach of its dealer agreements.

**3.2 List price reductions.** A reduction in published list price lowers the dealer MAP floor in the same proportion and therefore materially changes dealer economics. A list price reduction that lowers a product's MAP floor by more than **15%** requires Channel Director approval plus Legal review under section 6, and triggers the thirty day notice obligation in dealer agreement section 5.1.

**3.3** Direct-to-consumer-only products are not subject to this section.

## 4. Price change frequency

A given SKU's list price may be changed **no more than once per fiscal quarter**.

A price change takes effect on the first business day of the month following approval.

**4.1 Materiality.** A competitive gap of less than **5%** in either direction does not warrant a change to list price. Products inside that band are held. Gaps are measured against the comparable price as defined in section 1.

**4.2 Dealer notice.** Dealer agreement section 5.1 requires no less than thirty days written notice to the dealer before **any** change to the published list price of a dealer-channel product. This applies to increases and decreases alike, and to changes of any size.

The earliest effective date for a dealer-channel SKU is therefore the later of two dates: the first available date under section 5, and the expiry of the thirty day notice period.

**4.3 Gross profit convention.** Gross profit impact is reported as the change in gross profit: (new price minus current price) multiplied by period volume, holding volume and cost constant. A positive figure is additional gross profit. A negative figure is gross profit given up.

## 5. Promotional calendar

No list price change may take effect during a promotional blackout window. See `promo_calendar.md`.

Promotional pricing is a separate instrument from list price and is governed by the Marketing Promotions Standard, not by this policy.

## 6. Change approval

| Change type | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Price change within margin floor, no MAP impact | Category Manager |
| Price change on a dealer-channel SKU | Category Manager plus Channel Director |
| Price change breaching a margin floor | VP Commercial Finance plus written margin exception |
| List price reduction lowering the MAP floor by more than 15% | Channel Director plus Legal review |
| Any change to a SKU already repriced this quarter | Prohibited. See section 4. |

**6.1 Approvals are cumulative.** Where more than one row above applies to the same change, every listed approver is required. A margin exception granted under section 7 satisfies only the margin floor row. It does not substitute for Channel Director, Legal, or any other approval that is independently required.

**6.2** A change matching the "Prohibited" row may not proceed under any combination of approvals.

## 7. Exceptions

A margin exception may be granted for a period not exceeding two quarters, and must state the commercial rationale and the expected margin recovery path.

Exceptions are recorded in the quarterly pricing committee minutes.
